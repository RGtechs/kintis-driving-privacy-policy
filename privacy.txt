Advertising and Tracking

We use third-party services like Unity Ads for in-app advertising. These services may collect:

Why This Data Is Used:
- To serve personalized or contextual ads
- To measure ad performance
- To prevent fraud or misuse

You can manage ad tracking via device settings:
- Android: Settings → Google → Ads
- iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

If tracking is used, we follow Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. We will ask for your explicit permission before collecting any data used for tracking across apps or websites.
